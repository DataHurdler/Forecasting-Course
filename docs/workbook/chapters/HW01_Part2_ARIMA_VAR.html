17 Overview

This is Part 2 of Homework 1, due before class in Week 3. It assumes you have submitted Part 1 (exponential smoothing) and that you have your Part 1 accuracy table available — Q5 asks you to compare against it.

Part 2 covers ARIMA-based models and their multivariate extension, Vector Autoregression. Where exponential smoothing summarizes the past through a smoothed level, ARIMA models the correlation structure of the series directly, and VAR lets two series inform each other.

Learning objectives. By the end of Part 2 you will be able to:

  • Diagnose stationarity with the Augmented Dickey-Fuller test and difference a series appropriately
  • Identify a plausible ARIMA(\(p,d,q\)) order from ACF and PACF plots
  • Evaluate a model with walk-forward cross-validation rather than a single train/test split
  • Fit a VAR, run a Granger causality test, and state precisely what the result does and does not license you to claim
